This is personal for us

Peponi means paradise in Kiswahili.

For our family, Kenya has always been more than a destination. Shilen and Sahel were both born there, and although we now live in Australia, our connection to Kenya, its people and its wildlife has never left us.

Peponi Safaris exists to share the Kenya we know and love: personally, thoughtfully and with people who want more than a conventional safari.
